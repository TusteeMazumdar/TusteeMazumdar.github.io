**Authors:** Zeenath Reza Khan, Rokhaya Diagne, **Tustee Mazumdar**, Favour Chisom Akabogu, Kumari Neha Priya

**Abstract:** HAI-TRUST (Healthcare AI Trust Calibration Scenario Bank) is a structured research stimulus and instrument bank designed to study calibrated reliance on healthcare AI under incomplete information. It comprises 15 role-specific scenarios developed from five fictional healthcare contexts across three decision perspectives: public/patient-facing, healthcare/clinical, and AI/computing/developer. Each scenario is represented under six controlled AI output conditions: answer only, explanation, simulated confidence, uncertainty, warning/verification, and a combined output. The dataset also includes researcher-facing reference conditions and action-alignment coding to support experimental use. HAI-TRUST consists of fictional research stimuli. It is not a clinical dataset, medical advice, a diagnostic system, or a clinically validated decision-support resource. The empirical study associated with the project fielded version 0.9; version 1.0 is the post-review repository release and should not be described as the exact stimulus version used for previously collected data.
