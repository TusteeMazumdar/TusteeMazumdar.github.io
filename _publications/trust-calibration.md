Abstract: Trust calibration is critical for safe and effective human-AI collaboration in healthcare, particularly when AI systems operate under incomplete information. This paper presents a thematic narrative review of trust calibration in healthcare AI and develops a conceptual framework for understanding how AI output features can influence user trust and decision-making. The framework highlights the role of explanations, confidence indicators, uncertainty cues, and warning prompts in supporting appropriately calibrated trust. The work aims to provide an evidence-based foundation for evaluating trust calibration in patient-facing healthcare AI systems.
